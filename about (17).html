The Story Behind Le Bâtiment LLC

Monday morning,
first thing.

Michael Meesen · Julie De Rycke · Belgian Crafted

"Monday morning, first thing. That's the classic phrase of every true entrepreneur — and Michael Meesen was literally born on a Monday morning at exactly 7:15 AM."

The week was already starting at full speed for his parents, Ludo Meesen and Agnès Timmermans, both entrepreneurs themselves at the time.

Michael grew up in an environment where work, creativity, and ambition were part of everyday life. Shortly after opening their first furniture store in Lanaken in 1989, his parents launched their own furniture manufacturing and retail business.

Extremely close to his father, Michael's greatest dream from the age of four was simply to imitate him. More than toys or cartoons, what truly excited him was painting, building, scribbling numbers onto invoices, or even clocking in with his own children's time card.

The Journey

A life built on
doing the impossible

1985

Born on a Monday morning

Born in Belgium on April 15, 1985 at 7:15 AM. From his earliest years, Michael was raised in a family where business, hard work, and ambition were simply part of life. By age seven, he was already attending client meetings, visiting trade shows, and operating forklifts in the family's 3,000 m² factory — his real playground.

1991

A promise to his mother

On November 13, 1991, he became a big brother and promised his mother he would always look after his younger brother Frank. Having grown up surrounded by adults, workers, warehouses, and business meetings, Michael never truly felt at home in school classrooms — preferring instead the family factory floor.

2004

Licenses, wings & heavy loads

Standing 6'5" tall, barely twenty years old, Michael earned his commercial pilot license, boating licenses, and multiple heavy vehicle certifications. Passionate about aviation and navigation, he could pilot an airplane, captain a boat, and maneuver a 40-ton truck with equal ease. People often repeated that no one could do everything. Michael spent his life proving the opposite.

2004–19

Eurobounder & MMX-Press

For nearly fifteen years, he worked side by side with his father building Eurobounder — a Luxembourg-based express transport company serving major international carriers such as TNT, FedEx, and UPS. Eurobounder became an important player in the rise of e-commerce logistics across Europe. When his father retired in 2019, Michael took over independently and transformed it into MMX-Press Transport.

2021

Le Bâtiment LLC is born

Founded in January 2021, Le Bâtiment LLC was created as the culmination of this journey. A construction and renovation company unlike any other — built around transparency, speed, flexibility, and above all, a genuine passion for craftsmanship. Nothing is standardized. Every project is treated as a personal mission, designed and executed with the same care as if it were their own.

Today

Beyond borders

Today, Michael and Julie are no longer limited to Belgium. They travel internationally and have already completed projects and business concepts across the United States and overseas territories, with many more currently in development. Their vision goes beyond borders: bringing creativity, Belgian craftsmanship, and intelligent design wherever people seek spaces built with passion, precision, and purpose.

The Creative Director

Julie
De Rycke

Originally from Namur, Julie brings an entirely new dimension to the adventure. Passionate, dynamic, and highly creative, she combines the talents of an interior architect and home organizer with a rare ability to optimize and elevate spaces.

Deeply attentive to clients, she has the gift of transforming ideas into environments where aesthetics meet functionality and every visual detail contributes to a true sensory experience. Over the years, Julie expanded her expertise into the design of restaurants, retail spaces, and hospitality concepts.

Always driven by innovation, she also specialized in laser cleaning technology — mastering this forward-thinking field with the same precision she applies to her interior creations.
